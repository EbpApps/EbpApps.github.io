Ràdios de Catalunya privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In short

Ràdios de Catalunya is an app to listen to radio stations over the internet, made by EbpApps, an independent developer.

  • You don't need to create an account.
  • The app doesn't collect personal data or send it to any server of ours.
  • It has no ads and no usage statistics.
  • Your favourites, your history, your notes and your settings are only stored on your phone.

Data stored on your phone

To work, the app stores this data on the phone itself. Nobody else can access it, not even us:

  • Your favourite stations and the order you put them in.
  • The notes you write about the stations ("My notes").
  • The stations you have listened to: the last time, how long it lasted and the total listening time of each one.
  • Your settings: light or dark theme, colours, language, list order and the last time used in the sleep timer.
  • Two counters to ask you for a rating at a good moment: on how many different days you have listened to the radio and when we last asked you.
  • The equalizer settings of each station and its volume.
  • Your alarm clock alarms: time, days, station or sound, volume and, if you snoozed one, until when.
  • The history of updates of the station list: the date of each one and which stations changed.
  • A temporary copy of the station logos, so they aren't downloaded every time.

The "Clear history" button removes the stations from the list but keeps their listening times, so the total stays right if you listen to them again. To delete everything: phone Settings → Apps → Ràdios de Catalunya → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Android backup

If Google backup is turned on on your phone, Android may include this data in the backup of your Google account, to restore it if you change phones. It is managed by Google under its privacy policy, and you can turn it off in the phone settings.

Internet connections

To play a station, the app connects directly to that station's server (or to the platform it uses to broadcast). To show the logos, it downloads them from each station's website or from public radio image services.

As with any visit to a website, those servers receive the IP address of your connection and basic technical data (for example, the type of player). We don't send them anything else. Each station has its own privacy policy.

At most once a day, the app checks whether there is a newer station list and, if so, downloads it from our website (ebpapps.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages, by GitHub, Inc.). It is the same file for everyone: the app sends no data about you, it only asks for it. As with any website, GitHub receives the IP address of your connection and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Messages you choose to send

The "Suggest a station", "Report" (when a station doesn't work), "Report the correct link" and "Report wrong information about this station" options open your email app with a message already written. Nothing is sent until you tap "Send", and you can read and change it first.

The "Report" message includes technical data to fix the problem: the station's name and link, the error, the app and Android versions and the phone model. We use your email and your message only to answer you and improve the station catalogue, and we don't share them with anyone.

Google Play and other apps

  • Google Play: downloads, updates and the window to rate the app are handled by Google under its privacy policy.
  • Share: opens the Android share menu with a text; you choose who with.
  • Map: "Show location" opens Google Maps with the station's location (not yours).
  • Station website: opens in your browser.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: to play the radio and know if you are connected, so we can explain properly why a station doesn't play.
  • Notifications: to show the radio controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
  • Background playback and keeping the phone awake: so the radio keeps playing with the screen off.
  • Audio settings: for the equalizer and the volume of each station.
  • Exact alarms: so the alarm clock rings at the right time (on Android 14 or later you turn it on in the settings).
  • Starting when the phone turns on: only to schedule your alarms again after a restart.
  • Full screen: to show the alarm over the lock screen when it rings.

The app doesn't use your location, contacts, microphone, camera or files.

Children

The app is suitable for everyone and doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.

Your rights and contact

If you have emailed us and want to see, correct or delete that email, or have any question about this policy, write to us at ebpapps+radioscat@gmail.com. If you think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data in a different way (for example, if it ever shows ads), we will update this page before publishing that version and change the date above.
